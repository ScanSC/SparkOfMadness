    Paladin : Oath of Splendor

    The Oath of Splendor belongs to paladins who seek absolute perfection in both their might and their elegance. To them, every battle is a work of art in which mastery, grace, and brutality must harmonize perfectly. Through their enchanted armor, they can assume various forms of power, borrowing the attributes of the greatest creatures and supernatural forces.
    Unwavering in the face of adversity, these paladins refuse defeat, continuing to fight even when their bodies ought to give out.

    Tenets of Splendor

    Though the exact words and strictures of the Oath of Splendor vary, paladins of this oath share these tenets.
    - The pursuit of perfection. You can always do better, and you always strive to do so.
    - Never give up. You never accept defeat in a serious fight.
    - Elegance in violence. A fight is an artistic performance. Brute force without mastery is merely vulgarity, and victory is as important as the manner in which it is achieved.

    Channel Divinity

    When you take this oath at 3rd level, you gain the following two Channel Divinity options.

    Wardrobe of Splendor. As a bonus action, you can expend your Channel Divinity to imbue your armor with a blessing. For 1 minute, when you use this Channel Divinity or as a bonus action while it lasts, you can choose one of the blessings in the table below.
    The blessing ends any spell affecting the armor and prevents any spell from affecting it, except for spells granted by a blessing.

    Armor blessing Effects
    Angel of steel armor You gain a flying speed of 30 feet. When a creature hits you with a melee attack, you can use your reaction to cast the sword burst cantrip.
    Devil of steel armor You gain a flying speed of 30 feet. When your melee attack reduces a creature to hit points equal to or lower than your Charisma modifier, it dies. When you kill a creature, each other non-ally creature within 15 feet of you must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or become frightened of you until the end of its next turn.
    Armor of the Flame Empress You have resistance to fire damage. The first melee attack you make on each of your turns benefits from the green-flame blade cantrip.
    Armor of the Lightning Empress You have resistance to lightning damage. When you take the Attack action on your turn, you can replace one of your attacks with a casting of lightning lure.
    Armor of the Frozen Golem You have resistance to cold damage. At the beginning of your turn, ice forms around your armor, granting you temporary hit points equal to your proficiency bonus + your Charisma modifier until the start of your next turn.
    Armor of the Sea King You have resistance to acid damage and a swimming speed of 30 feet. When a creature hits you, you can use your reaction to cast acid splash at that creature.
    Armor of the Cloud Giant You can't be moved against your will, and difficult terrain doesn't cost you extra movement. Your Strength modifier increases by your Charisma modifier.
    Mithril rampart You gain the benefits of blade ward. Your body can protect other creatures from directional area effects, such as fireball and lightning bolt.
    Clear Heart You lose the bonus to Armor Class granted by your armor, but you add that amount to any ability check or saving throw you make using Dexterity or Wisdom.
    Armor of Feline Rush Your walking speed increases by 20 feet, and moving doesn't provoke opportunity attacks.
    Robe of the Archfey You can cast misty step by expending a spell slot. You are immune to the charmed condition. Whenever a creature deals damage to you, you can use your reaction to force it to make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, the creature is charmed by you until the start of your next turn.
    Armor of the Morning Star You have resistance to radiant damage, and your armor sheds light as if affected by the light spell. Your melee attacks can become ranged attacks with a range of 30 feet, using Dexterity for the attack and damage rolls, and deal radiant damage, as you throw waves of light with your blade.
    Armor of the Destiny You have advantage on saving throws against spells. The first time on each of your turns that you hit a creature with a melee attack, you break its concentration, and it is silenced until the end of its next turn.

    The blessing can change the armor's appearance, but it doesn't fool someone trying to determine whether you are wearing armor.

    You can end this effect on your turn (no action required). When the effect ends, your armor returns to its original state.

    Inspiring Speech. You can make a short monologue or song, your words clinging to the mind of one creature of your choice that can hear you, to help it approach perfection. As an action, you make a Performance check. The creature can add half the result to any ability check it makes for 1 minute or until it fails an ability check.

    Aura of Perfection

    Starting at 7th level, whenever you or an ally within 10 feet of you hits with an attack roll, the attack deals extra damage equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum of +1). The extra damage is of the same type as the attack. You must be conscious to grant this bonus. At 18th level, the range of this aura increases to 30 feet.

    Too Formidable

    Beginning at 15th level, when you would make a death saving throw, you can instead rise one last time with 1 hit point. Until the end of your next turn, your speed is doubled, you can't be restrained, you ignore the effects of exhaustion, you are immune to all damage, and all your attacks are critical hits.
    When the effect ends, you gain 3 levels of exhaustion.
