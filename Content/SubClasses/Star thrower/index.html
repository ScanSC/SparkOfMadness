    Ranger : Star Thrower

    Rangers of this conclave read the night sky as both map and arsenal. By binding thrown weapons to a chosen constellation, they call down flashes of celestial power on their quarry and recall their star-marked arms with a gesture.

    Magical Constellation

    By performing a 1-hour ritual, you can magically bind up to six weapons to a constellation. The weapons must have the thrown property.
    When you hit a creature with a ranged weapon attack using a bound weapon, the weapon attaches to the target, floating around it and following it. If the target is teleported or changes planes, the weapons detach and return to you.
    If a target has six bound weapons attached to it, it triggers the constellation's Star Fall, and the weapons return to you.

    Constellation Name Star Fall
    Rose The target takes 2d10 radiant + 2d10 poison damage, and is charmed by you for a number of turns equal to your proficiency bonus
    Anvil The target takes 2d10 radiant + 2d10 force damage, and must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or become stunned for a number of turns equal to half your proficiency bonus (rounded down)
    Claw The target takes 2d10 radiant + 2d10 necrotic damage, and is bleeding for a number of turns equal to your proficiency bonus
    Phoenix The target takes 2d10 radiant + 2d10 fire damage, and is burning for a number of turns equal to your proficiency bonus

    Shooting Star

    At 3rd level, when you take the Attack action and throw a bound weapon, you can use a bonus action to throw another bound weapon at the same target.

    Shiny Recall

    At 3rd level, as a bonus action, you can recall all your thrown weapons. For each recalled weapon that was attached to a creature, that creature takes radiant damage equal to your Wisdom modifier.

    Lucky Star

    At 7th level, when you miss with a thrown weapon attack using a bound weapon, you can still attach the weapon to the target.
    You can use this trait a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus + 1.
    You regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

    Star Light

    At 11th level, when you hit a creature with a thrown weapon attack using a bound weapon, you deal additional damage equal to your Wisdom modifier.
    Moreover, when you trigger Star Fall: - the target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or become blinded until the end of its next turn; - you can expend a spell slot to add 1d10 radiant damage for each level of the slot.

    Light Speed

    At 15th level, using an action, you can throw all of your bound weapons at once (make separated attack rolls). Your walking speed is doubled and you don't trigger opportunity attacks until the end of your turn.
