    Druid : Circle of the Cauldron

    These druids are rarely found together, as they more or less detest one another—a sentiment stemming from an ancient rite that involved presenting one’s latest alchemical inventions to the rest of the group, and the last iteration of this event proved to be explosive. Druids from other circles mock them, claiming that the members of the Circle of the Cauldron had a falling out over a cooking contest.

    Circle Spells

    When you join this circle at 2nd level, you access to some spells when you reach certain levels in this class, as shown on the Circle of the Cauldron Spells table.

    Once you gain access to one of these spells, you always have it prepared, and it doesn't count against the number of spells you can prepare each day. If you gain access to a spell that doesn't appear on the Druid Spell List, the spell is nonetheless a druid spell for you.

    Druid Level Spells
    2nd Catapult, Tasha's Caustic Brew
    3rd Barkskin, Alter Self
    5th Plant Growth, Gaseous Form
    7th Polymorph, Sickening Radiance
    9th Immolation, Contagion

    Alchemy

    At 2nd level, you learn to create brews from plants you magically grow. You know a number of recipes equal to half your druid level. Whenever you gain a level in this class and don't learn a new recipe, you can replace one recipe you know with another one.

    Recipe Druid Level Required Usage Effect
    Healing Elixir 2nd Drink A creature that drinks this elixir regains 2d4 + your Wisdom modifier hit points.
    Charming Perfume 2nd Spread You apply the perfume to one creature. For 10 minutes, any creature that ends its turn within 5 feet of the perfumed creature must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or become charmed by the perfumed creature until the start of its next turn.
    Sticky Mixture 2nd Area When the flask breaks, it covers a 20-foot-radius area with a sticky substance that creates difficult terrain.
    Explosive Oil 2nd Area When the flask breaks, it covers the ground in a 10-foot-radius area with an explosive mixture. If the surface takes any fire damage, it explodes.     Smoke Bomb 2nd Area When the flask breaks, it creates a cloud of smoke in a 10-foot radius. The smoke blocks line of sight, and each creature in the area is blinded while there. The smoke lasts for 1 minute.
    Tempest in a Bottle 2nd Area When the flask breaks, it releases a burst of wind in a 5-foot radius. Each creature in the area must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 10 feet away from the center of the blast.
    Restorative Potion 6th Drink A creature that drinks this potion gains the benefits of the lesser restoration spell.
    Gnar's Wrath 6th Drink A creature that drinks this potion transforms into a Huge beast for 1 minute. Its size increases by one category, it gains claws that deal 1d4 + its Strength modifier slashing damage (unarmed strike), and its Strength modifier increases by your Wisdom modifier. It can make an unarmed strike as a bonus action. When the effect ends, the creature gains one level of exhaustion.
    Auril's Grasp 6th Spread A creature affected by the potion must succeed on a Strength saving throw or become restrained by ice. The creature can repeat the saving throw at the beginning of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. The effect lasts for up to 1 minute.
    Hiatea's Embrace 6th Spread A creature affected by the potion is encased in an icy cocoon and gains resistance to all damage. The cocoon grants the creature temporary hit points equal to 1d4 × (your Wisdom modifier + 1). While the cocoon lasts, the creature regains 2d4 hit points at the end of each of its turns. The effect lasts for up to 1 minute. The creature can leave the cocoon by using a bonus action.
    Bomb 6th Area When the flask breaks, it explodes in a 5-foot radius. Each creature in the explosion must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking 1d4 force + 2d4 fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
    Talona's Gas 6th Area When the flask breaks, it unleashes a greenish cloud in a 5-foot radius. Each creature in the cloud or that ends its turn there must make a Constitution saving throw, taking 2d4 poison damage and becoming poisoned until the end of its next turn on a failed save, or taking half as much damage on a successful one.
    Dryad's Kiss 14th Spread A creature hit by the flask must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or become Petrified until the end of its next turn and gain vulnerability to fire damage for the duration.
    Nala's Bubbles 14th Area When the flask breaks, it unleashes a cloud of small bubbles in a 20-foot radius. The entire area is treated as underwater, except for the purposes of movement, and is difficult terrain.
    Smell of Madness 14th Area When the flask breaks, it creates a cloud of purple smoke in a 10-foot radius that lasts for 3 rounds. A creature that can breathe and starts its turn in the cloud must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or regard every other creature as hostile until the start of its next turn.
    Infernal Megabomb 14th Area When the flask breaks, it explodes in a 10-foot radius. Each creature in the explosion must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking 2d4 force + 3d4 fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

    You can carry a number of potions equal to your druid level + your Wisdom modifier.
    During a long rest, you can replenish your supply of potions if you have access to soil in which to grow the plants. You choose any combination of potions to carry. Each 14th-level potion requires a spell slot of 1st level or higher to create, and a higher-level spell slot allows you to create more of them.

    The saving throw DC for your potions equals your spell save DC.

    A creature hit by a thrown potion takes 1 bludgeoning damage.

    Humanoid Artillery

    At 6th level, you become better at throwing potions. When you make an attack roll to throw one of your flasks, you add your Wisdom modifier to the roll.

    In addition, you can throw a flask and cast a spell as part of the same action, provided the spell's level is one lower than the level of your highest-level spell slot.

    Empowered Brewings

    At 10th level, your potions use d8s instead of d4s.

    Secret Ingredient

    At 14th level, a creature that makes a saving throw as a result of one of your potions has disadvantage on that saving throw.
    In addition, you automatically succeed on saving throws you make as a result of your own potions.
