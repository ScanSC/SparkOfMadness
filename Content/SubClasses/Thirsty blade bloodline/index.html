    Sorcerer : Thirsty blade bloodline

    It is said that these sorcerers placed a portion of their soul into their weapon. The blade answers its master's call, sharing in the power and the hunger that comes with their bond.

    Pact of the thirst

    At 1st level, by performing a 1-hour ritual, you can bond a melee weapon to yourself. This weapon becomes your Thirsty Blade until you die or bond with another weapon.
    A Thirsty Blade can't receive any pact magic or be used for bladesong.
    The Thirsty Blade uses your Charisma modifier and gains the following benefits:

    • You are proficient with it.

    • Call of the Hunt. As a bonus action, you can teleport it to your hand and equip it. If your hand isn't free, the object in that hand is teleported to where the Thirsty Blade was.

    • Corrupted Blade. When you hit with it, you can spend 1 sorcery point to add a number of d6s equal to your proficiency bonus in necrotic damage.

    • Vampiric Feast. When you hit a non-construct creature with it, you regain a number of hit points equal to your Charisma modifier. If the attack kills the target, you regain 1 sorcery point and increase the healing by the excess damage dealt.

    Sorcerer's trick

    At 1st level, you can use your Metamagic on attacks made with your Thirsty Blade as though the blade were a 1st-level melee spell.

    Unstoppable thirst

    At 6th level, if you fall unconscious, your Thirsty Blade attacks on its own once per turn. It targets creatures within its reach, prioritizing enemies before other creatures and creatures with fewer hit points before those with more.
    These attacks use your Charisma modifier and proficiency bonus and trigger Vampiric Feast.
    The blade can't make opportunity attacks on its own.

    Great thirst

    At 14th level, your Thirsty Blade becomes more powerful. Corrupted Blade also prevents the target from being healed until the end of your next turn, and Vampiric Feast restores twice as many hit points.

    Thirsty Blade master

    At 18th level, your Thirsty Blade is a part of you, and attacking with it is so natural that you can cast a spell while attacking. After you make an attack with your Thirsty Blade, you can cast any spell that normally requires an action as a bonus action instead.
