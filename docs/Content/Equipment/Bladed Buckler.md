# Weapon : Bladed Buckler

A small shield attached to the forearm. The front of the shield joins into a wide, sharp blade.

__Cost__ : 12 gp<br>
__Damage__ : <span style="color:lightslategrey">1d6 slashing</span><br>
__Weight__ : 5 lb<br>
__Properties__ : Light, Finesse, +1 AC

Although this weapon doesn't need to be held in your hand, it hinders you if you try to use a weapon in that hand. If you do so, the weapon in that hand doesn't benefit from your proficiency bonus.

This weapon counts as both a weapon and a shield.
