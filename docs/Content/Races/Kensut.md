# Kensut

*These humanoid foxes originate from the south of Faerûn and have long been outcasts. Most often working as merchants or highwaymen, they are particularly at home in desert regions.*<br>
*Long ago, one of them became wealthy enough to construct a great vessel, allowing him to migrate to the nearby island of Samargol, taking as many of his kin along with him as possible.*

- __Ability Score Increase__ : When determining your character’s ability scores, increase one score by 2 and increase a different score by 1, or increase three different scores by 1. You can't raise any of your scores above 20.

- __Age__ : Kensuts have lifespans equivalent to humans.

- __Alignment__ : Kensuts tend toward chaotic alignments, as impulse and fancy guide their decisions. They are often nomads who live by commerce or become highwaymen.

- __Size__ : You are Medium or Small. You choose the size when you select this race.

- __Speed__ : Your walking speed is 30 feet, and you have a climbing speed of 20 feet.

- __Fox Cunning__ : You have proficiency in Deception and Stealth skills. When rolling a Deception check, add your intelligence modifier (minimum 0) to the roll.

- __Desert Wanderer__ : You have been accustomed to deserts and [sandstorms](../../09-Other/Sandstorm.md) since childhood. While in a sandstorm, you have advantage on saving throws against its effects and can see through it to a distance of 30 feet.
You also don't suffer adverse effects from high temperatures in the desert.

- __Languages__ : Your character can speak, read, and write Common and one other language that you and your DM agree is appropriate for the character. The Player’s Handbook offers a list of languages to choose from. The DM is free to modify that list for a campaign.
