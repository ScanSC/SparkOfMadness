# Barbarian : path of the anvil

Barbarians who follow the Path of the Anvil temper their fury like iron in a forge. Their rage kindles a searing inner heat, turning each blow into a hammer strike and making them stubborn defenders who grow more dangerous the longer a battle rages.

### Armored Defense (Replaces Unarmored Defense)
At 3rd level, you gain proficiency with heavy armor and smith's tools.<br>
While raging, you add your Constitution modifier to your AC and have advantage on Constitution saving throws.

### Molten fist
At 3rd level, while raging, your melee attacks deal extra <span style="color:darkorange">fire</span> damage equal to your Constitution modifier.

### Burning rage
At 6th level, while raging, you have resistance to <span style="color:darkorange">fire</span> damage. As a bonus action, you can unleash a burst of flames. Each creature within 5 feet of you takes <span style="color:darkorange">1d4 + your Constitution modifier fire</span> damage.

### Erratic flames
At 10th level, __Burning Rage__ frees you from the <span style="color:limegreen">restrained</span> and grappled conditions.
Additionally, when you use a bonus action to end your rage, you can release your inner fire. Each creature (except yourself) in a 10-foot-radius burst takes a number of d10s of <span style="color:darkorange">fire</span> damage equal to your proficiency bonus.

### Wrath of the forge
At 14th level, while raging, when an enemy hits you with a melee attack, it takes <span style="color:darkorange">2d4 + your Constitution modifier fire</span> damage.<br>
Moreover, __Molten Fist__ and __Burning Rage__ deal one additional <span style="color:darkorange">d4 fire</span> damage.
