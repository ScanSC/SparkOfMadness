# Rogue : Card Bettor

Legend has it that the first "Card Bettor" was an assassin so gifted that every contract he took on bored him. One evening, while drinking and playing cards with a magician friend, the two came up with a brilliant idea.\
This combat method did not meet with much success, as the assassin died during his very next contract, having drawn the wrong card. Nevertheless, those who still practice it have sown panic among a group of mentalist bandits.

### Cards Conjuration
You have proficiency with card games.
At 3rd level, you learn to create magical duplicates from cards. You must have the original card on you to duplicate it. If it's an __Arcanic card__, it must be part of your __deck__.\
You can throw those duplicates, using your Dexterity modifier for the attack roll and dealing <span style="color:lightslategrey">1d6 slashing damage</span> (without adding your Dexterity modifier). The conjuration can be part of a ranged weapon attack.

### Arcanic Card Deck
At 3rd level, you learn to identify if a card is magical or not.\
During a 1-hour ritual, you can imbue your mind with exactly six different __Arcanic cards__. You also assign one number from 1 to 6 to each card.\
Arcanic cards deal bonus damage and can have an __amplified effect__.

When you hit with an __Arcanic card__, you announce a number between 1 and 6. The first damage roll (<span style="color:lightslategrey">1d6 slashing damage</span>) determines the thrown card (by its assigned number). If it matches the announced number, it triggers the __amplified effect__.\
If you hit with your __Sneak Attack__, double the bonus damage from the __Arcanic card__.\
You start with 6 of these cards in your inventory :
Card | Bonus Damages | Amplified Effect
-----|---------------|-----------------

The __Arcanic__ cards DC use your intelligence modifier.

### Fast draw
At 9th level, when you use your action to throw a card, you can throw two cards at two different targets using the same attack roll.

### Stacked deck
At 13th level, you add one additional d6 to the damage of each __Arcanic card__.\
Moreover, once per short rest, you can choose the result of a card's damage roll.

### Very fast draw
At 17th level, __Fast Draw__ can throw a card at a third target.
