# Bard : School of the Cascade

The magic of the bards of the School of the Cascade manifests as sparkling, pure water that leaps from creature to creature, healing wounds and easing torments.

### Revitalizing Water
At 3rd level, when you restore hit points, the healing manifests as flying __water orbs__.\
As an action, you can conjure up to 1 liter of water.

### Expanded Spell List
The following spells are added to the bard spell list for you:
Spell level | Spells |
------------|--------|
0 | Shape water
1 | Create or destroy water
2 | Wall of Water
3 | Water Breathing
3 | Water Walk
4 | Control Water
4 | Watery Sphere

Those spells don't count against the number of spells you know.

### Art of the Cascade
At 3rd level, when you restore at least 2 hit points to a single target with a spell, the __water orb__ can bounce to another target within 15 feet and restore half the original amount (rounded down).\
__Art of the Cascade__ can trigger itself.

### Shield of Water
At 6th level, while a creature has one of your __bardic inspirations__, it is surrounded by a thin sphere of water, granting it resistance to <span style="color:darkorange">fire</span> and <span style="color:greenyellow">acid</span> damage and the ability to breathe underwater.\
When a creature consumes the bardic inspiration, the shield explodes. Creatures within 5 feet regain hit points equal to the number rolled on the bardic inspiration die.\
If a creature protected by your __Shield of Water__ is healed by __Art of the Cascade__, a splash of healing water erupts, and all creatures within 5 feet regain hit points equal to your Charisma modifier.

### Inspired Cares
At 14th level, when you use a healing spell, you can expend a __bardic inspiration__ to restore the maximum possible number of hit points. This option can be used with multi-target features but affects only one target.\
You can use this feature during __Art of the Cascade__, and it affects the amount healed by the feature that triggered the cascade.
