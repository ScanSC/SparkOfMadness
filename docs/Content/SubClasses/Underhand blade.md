# Rogue : Underhand Blade

Some people will do anything to win a fight. Rogues who follow the Underhand Blade know that honor is of no use to the dead.

### Cunning Tricks
You gain proficiency in the Deception skill.
At 3rd level, you can use various tricks to gain an unfair advantage in combat. As a bonus action, you can use one of the following tricks:
- **Sand Toss.** You throw sand in an enemy's face. The target must be within 5 feet of you, and you make an attack roll using Charisma. On a hit, the target takes <span style="color:lightslategrey">1d4 bludgeoning damage</span>. It must also succeed on a Constitution saving throw or become <span style="color:ivory">blinded</span> until the end of its next turn. You must have a free hand.
- **Disarm.** You swing your blade and attempt to strike your enemy's weapon hand. Make a melee weapon attack. On a hit, the target takes <span style="color:lightslategrey">1d6 slashing damage</span>. It must also succeed on a Strength saving throw or drop the weapon. If the target holds the weapon with two hands, it has advantage on the saving throw.
- **Leg Trip.** You attempt to knock your enemy prone by striking its legs. The target must be within 5 feet of you, and you make an attack roll. On a hit, the target takes <span style="color:lightslategrey">1d6 bludgeoning damage</span> and must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or fall prone. The target must be your size or smaller.
- **Forbidden Strike.** You attempt to strike your enemy in the abdomen or groin. The target must be within 5 feet of you, and you make an attack roll. On a hit, the target takes <span style="color:lightslategrey">1d6 bludgeoning damage</span> and must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or have its speed reduced to 0 until the end of its next turn.
- **Devious Mockery.** You mock your opponent with total contempt. Cast *vicious mockery*.
The saving throw DC for your Cunning Tricks equals 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Charisma modifier.

### Opportunistic
At 3rd level, you have advantage on attack rolls against enemies affected by one of your Cunning Tricks.

### Honour is a Foreign Concept
At 9th level, you master the art of deception in battle. When you take nonlethal damage, you can fake death and fall prone. While in this state, your next attack roll or opportunity attack has advantage, but it reveals your deception. Standing up uses movement normally.
If you take nonlethal damage while faking death, you must succeed on a Constitution saving throw (DC 8 + half the damage) or cry out and reveal your deception while remaining prone.
An enemy won't fall for this trick twice and may try to kill you.

### Master of Deceit
At 13th level, you know where to strike even heavily armored enemies. Your attacks ignore resistance to nonmagical slashing, piercing, and bludgeoning damage.
You also gain expertise in Deception.

### Underhand Expert
At 17th level, you double your Charisma modifier when calculating the DC of a Cunning Trick, and you add your Charisma modifier to damage dealt by a Cunning Trick.
