# Druid : Circle of the Displacer

*Druids of the Circle of the Displacer are as rare as the beasts they admire—and just as formidable.*

### Hunter Wild Shape
When you choose this circle at 2nd level, you gain the ability to use Wild Shape on your turn as a bonus action, rather than as an action.<br>
Additionally, while you are transformed by Displacer Shapes, you add the beast's Dexterity modifier to its AC.

### Displacer Shapes
At 2nd level, using Wild Shape, you gain the ability to transform into a displacer beast. At higher levels, you unlock more powerful shapes.

Level Required | Shape
---------------|------
2 | [Displacer Cub](../../08-Monster/Fiches/Displacer%20Cub.md)
6 | [Young Displacer Beast](../../08-Monster/Fiches/Young%20Displacer%20Beast.md)
10 | [Displacer Beast](../../08-Monster/Fiches/Displacer%20Beast.md)
14 | [Ancient Displacer Beast](../../08-Monster/Fiches/Ancient%20Displacer%20Beast.md)
18 | [Mythic Displacer Beast](../../08-Monster/Fiches/Mythic%20Displacer%20Beast.md)

At 18th level, while you are transformed by Displacer Shapes, you can only cast illusion spells with Beast Spells feature.

### A Trail of Fear
At 6th level, you are accustomed to nature and hunting. You gain proficiency in Survival (or expertise if you are already proficient), and you can smell the exact direction of a creature you have <span style="color:darkorchid">frightened</span> during the last 24 hours, up to 1000 feet.

### Mental Crack
At 10th level, your <span style="color:hotpink">psychic</span> damage ignores resistance.<br>
Moreover, while you are hidden from a target, you double your proficiency bonus when determining the DC of your spells that require a Wisdom saving throw.

### Displacer friend
At 14th level, displacer beasts become friendly toward you, and you gain the ability to communicate with them.<br>
Moreover, you can cast the __Displacement__ feature as a 2nd-level spell. For each level of upcasting, the feature deals an additional <span style="color:hotpink">2d4 psychic damage</span>.
